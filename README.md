**Nobody told you Haley hates Prismatic Shards. Now you know, and now it's gone.**

In vanilla Stardew Valley, handing Haley (or any NPC) something they hate is a one-way trip. The item is gone, the friendship tanks, and you are left wondering why you thought she would be any different. **[No Thanks, Keep It](https://www.nexusmods.com/stardewvalley/mods/45862)** changes that. When you gift her something she hate or dislike, she hand it right back. The friendship penalty still applies, you still made the mistake, but at least you walk away with your Prismatic Shard.

### How it Works
- Attempt to give an NPC an item they hate or dislike.
- The full gifting interaction plays out normally. The NPC reacts, friendship changes, and the gift counts toward your daily and weekly limit.
- The item is returned to your inventory.

### Features
1. **Hated Gift Return**  
  By default, items an NPC hates are returned to your inventory after gifting. The friendship penalty still applies.
2. **Optional Disliked Gift Return**  
  Disliked items are opt-in. Leave it off if you prefer to learn NPC preferences the hard way. Enable it if you already know and just want your item back.
3. **Fully Vanilla Friendly**  
  The gifting interaction is untouched. Animations, dialogue, friendship penalties, and daily and weekly gift limits all behave exactly as the base game intended. The only difference is the item ends up back in your pocket.

### Configuration
Fully compatible with **[Generic Mod Config Menu](https://www.nexusmods.com/stardewvalley/mods/5098)**. Accessible from the title screen or in-game:
- **Enable Mod** (_true_): Turn the mod on or off entirely.
- **Reject Hated Gifts** (_true_): Return items that an NPC hates back to your inventory.
- **Reject Disliked Gifts** (_false_): Return items that an NPC dislikes back to your inventory.

_Note: Regardless of configuration, the friendship penalty always applies and the gift always counts toward your daily and weekly limit. You still made the mistake, you just get the item back._

### Installation
1. Install the latest version of [SMAPI](https://smapi.io/).
2. (Optional but recommended) Install **Generic Mod Config Menu** to configure the mod in-game.
3. Download this mod and unzip it into your `Stardew Valley/Mods` folder.
4. Launch the game using SMAPI.

### Uninstallation
Delete the mod folder from your `Stardew Valley/Mods` directory. Safe to remove mid-playthrough with no side effects.

### Known Limitations
- Multiplayer has not been formally tested. The patch is scoped per-call and per-farmer, so it should behave correctly, if problem arise, please file a bug report.

### Bug Reports
